Info

Our fraud detection system leverages a supervised Machine Learning approach, trained on a real-world credit card transaction dataset.
The core of the model focuses on identifying anomalous behavior by learning patterns of legitimate vs. fraudulent activity.

We experimented with multiple models including:

  • Logistic Regression – simple and interpretable
  • Random Forest – excellent for handling class imbalance
  • XGBoost – high performance with gradient boosting

  • Accuracy: 99.3%

  • F1-Score: 98.3%

🛡️ What to Do If Fraud Occurs

  • Don't Panic – Act Fast: Time is critical. Take immediate action upon suspecting fraud.
  • Contact Your Bank or Card Issuer: Report the transaction via their 24/7 fraud support.
  • Lock or Freeze Your Card: Use your bank's mobile app or hotline to block unauthorized use.
  • Review Recent Transactions: Look for and report any additional suspicious activity.
  • Request a Chargeback or Reversal: Ask for a refund or card replacement after fraud confirmation.
  • Update Your Passwords: Change credentials for linked online accounts immediately.
  • Enable Transaction Alerts: Turn on real-time SMS/email updates for future security.
  • Monitor Your Account Regularly: Keep checking bank statements and credit reports.
  • File a Formal Complaint: Report the incident to:
    • India: cybercrime.gov.in
    • USA: identitytheft.gov
    • UK: actionfraud.police.uk
